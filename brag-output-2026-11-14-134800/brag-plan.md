# Brag Plan: Dyslexia Assistant

## What is this app?
An offline-first, neurodivergent-tailored reading, writing, and cognitive learning workspace that replaces chaotic letter perception with weighted typography, tactile paper-cutout tiles, calming Irlen tints, bionic fixation anchors, and spaced repetition.

## The angle
Dyslexia isn't a limitation; it's a perceptual difference that standard software ignores. Dyslexia Assistant proves that accessible design doesn't mean ugly, stripped-down utilities—it can be modern, beautiful, ultra-fast, and 100% private.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Visual representation of letter crowding and disorientation ("1 in 5 people experiences text like this"), snapping into crisp, stabilized, weighted letterforms as an eye-tracking focal grid locks into place.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Adaptive Typefaces & Ransom Collage**: Live cycling between OpenDyslexic (weighted bottoms), Lexend (reading flow), and tactile Ransom Note collage paper tiles with paper rotations and realistic drop shadows.
- **Bionic Reading & Word-Tracking TTS**: Text illuminated with bold fixation anchors while a glowing word-tracking indicator glides smoothly across calming Sage Green and Sepia paper tints.
- **Cognitive Writer & 7-Interval SRS**: Real-time Flesch-Kincaid readability scoring, phonics spell correction, and interactive 3D flashcards targeting common letter confusions (b vs d).

## Outro / punchline
"Read Freely. Write Confidently. 100% Offline · Zero Telemetry · Open Source."

## User flow worth showing
Entry on clean reader surface → Upload/Paste document → Select Ransom Note / OpenDyslexic font → Activate Bionic Reading + Sage Green Tint → Trigger ambient focus noise → Study letter confusions via Spaced Repetition flashcards.
