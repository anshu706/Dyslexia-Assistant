# Brag Plan: Dyslexia Assistant

## What is this app?
An offline-first, neurodivergent-tailored reading, writing, and cognitive learning workspace that dissolves ocular fatigue and letter jitter with Irlen spectral tints, specialized neuro-typographies, bionic fixation, and synchronized speech tracking.

## The angle
"The rhythm of reading changes the moment the page stops fighting your eyes."
Instead of treating reading difficulties as a struggle with personal discipline, Dyslexia Assistant treats screens as poorly calibrated instruments and recalibrates them for how neurodivergent brains actually decode type.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A high-contrast stark white screen with jittering letters suddenly softens into calming Sage Green with tactile paper grid and HUD coordinates, followed by the bold statement: "Dyslexia Assistant: A quieter way to read."

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Spectral Glare Relief (02-07s):** Live swatch cycling across clinical Irlen tints (Sage Green, Sky Blue, Soft Cream, Lavender) with instant atmospheric tint transformation.
2. **Neuro-Typographic Engine (07-12s):** Side-by-side fixation mechanics: toggling Lexend and OpenDyslexic, activating Bionic bolding on initial syllables, and phonetic hyphenation (`Syl·la·ble`).
3. **Synchronized Word-Boundary Speech & Reading Rulers (12-16s):** Precision TTS with glowing elevated Word Focus Box tracking each word in real-time alongside laser reading guides.

## Outro / punchline (16-20s)
"Zero Glare. 100% Offline. 100% Private."
Clean final brand card with folder tabs and "Launch Workspace".

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** Select calming Irlen tint (Sage Green / Sky Blue) to eliminate screen glare.
2. **Key action:** Enable Bionic Fixation & OpenDyslexic font on custom reading text.
3. **Result:** Synchronized speech audio with real-time magnified word-boundary tracking box.
