- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: "Calm, tactile assistive reading instrument — architectural paper meets neurodivergent precision"
- Interpretation: Thoughtful, serene pacing with tactile micro-interactions, clean holds, and high legibility.
- Hook: High contrast glare softening into soothing Sage Green with HUD coordinate tracker.
- Outro: Architectural closing card with offline privacy badges and "Launch Workspace".

1. Scene 1 — The Glare vs The Calm (0.00s - 5.00s) — Stark contrast resolves to Sage Green Irlen tint; HUD coordinates activate.
2. Scene 2 — Specialized Neuro-Typography (5.00s - 10.50s) — Lexend vs OpenDyslexic glyphs, Bionic reading saccade bolding.
3. Scene 3 — The Active Reading Workspace (10.50s - 16.00s) — Reader Sheet in action with real document parser, reading ruler, and synchronized word box.
4. Scene 4 — Zero Glare, 100% Offline (16.00s - 20.00s) — Privacy badges, offline PWA capability, closing CTA.
